# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Motion for React, and Lucide icons, as specified in the build brief. Deploy target is Netlify when a deploy is requested; this portfolio stays static and does not add a backend, CMS, auth, or database.

## Users

People evaluating Aryan Jagtap for design and product work: hiring managers, design peers, and collaborators who open the portfolio to understand who he is, what he has shipped, and how to reach him.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that presents Aryan’s current role, two replaceable case studies, a truthful career timeline, tools, resume, and contact paths. Success is a visitor who can read the work, open a case study, and copy a real email or download a real resume once those assets are supplied.

## Positioning

A compact bento portfolio for one designer. It shows only verified career facts and clearly labeled demo projects until Aryan replaces them with his own case studies.

## Operating Context

Desktop-first reading of a single homepage, an About page, and case-study routes. The homepage is the directory; project and About pages are secondary reading surfaces. Content is edited in one typed file plus local image and PDF assets.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Homepage bento, About page, and `/work/[slug]` case studies.
- Light theme by default, with a persistent light/dark switch.
- Exactly four contact destinations: LinkedIn, Instagram, Behance, and email.
- Resume view and download only when a real PDF is present.
- Experience timeline supports many roles, and shows a duration only when dates exist.
- Confirmed role: Senior Associate UX Designer at Digit Insurance, Bangalore. Start date unknown.
- Email, social URLs, portrait, resume PDF, earlier jobs, and real case studies are not supplied. The site must not invent them.
- No resource catalog, newsletter, pricing, testimonials, contact form, or conventional navigation bar.
- Do not deploy unless Aryan asks.

## Brand Commitments

The visual and structural reference is the Bentox bento portfolio: compact asymmetric cards, quiet DM Sans typography, generous radii, and image-led work tiles. Light is the first-visit theme. Frosted surfaces and the experience timeline are requested adaptations of that reference. Copy speaks as Aryan, in a calm, precise voice, and never as the template’s original designer.

## Evidence on Hand

- Name, display name, headline, Bangalore location, and Digit Insurance role.
- About draft copy supplied in the build brief.
- Two demo project frames (personal finance and job search), labeled as hypothetical until replaced.
- No portrait, email, social URLs, resume, employment dates, metrics, or personal photos.

## Product Principles

- Show only facts that were provided; mark everything else as a placeholder or demo.
- One content module owns personal data, projects, and career records.
- The portfolio should stay readable if motion or theme scripts fail.
- Case studies are structured sections that can be rewritten without changing the page layout.
- Reachability (email, resume, socials) stays honest when configuration is missing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, named controls, contrast that stays readable in both themes, reduced-motion support, and no essential labels that exist only on hover.
